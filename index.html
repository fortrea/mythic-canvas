Cursor Swarms

Five living canvas scenes in three worlds. A swarm follows your cursor, rings around it, performs formations when you go idle, and lives through a full day–night cycle.

Heavenly mountains · originalBone DragonsSkeleton dragons that grow celestial skins and long beards, then ascend into stars. Heavenly mountains · celestialCelestial DragonsEastern dragons in royal starry mantles that materialize from starlight, chase glowing pearls and ascend into stars. Heavenly mountains · realisticRealistic DragonsRichly scaled dragons with painted heads, horns and whiskers. The royal mantle can be switched on or off. Toxic forestToxic MushroomsPoisonous mushroom creatures that ripen, spit spores and reseed the forest floor. Village & old townCelestial MaidensWinged maidens in flowing gowns whose colors bloom as they circle you.
Move the cursor to lead the swarm · tap or click to trigger their move · stay still for formations
